About Aarthi Travels

Aarthi Travels is a travel service based in Sankarankovil, Tamil Nadu, offering vehicle rental options for family outings, group journeys, sightseeing, temple visits, and outstation travel.

Who We Are

Aarthi Travels is a travel agency in Sankarankovil serving families, friends, temple groups and organisations that need a dependable vehicle for a journey. We operate from 68 Mullai Nagar, 2nd Street, Sankarankovil, Tamil Nadu, and our tagline says it simply: Lets Go Travel.

Our work is built around one idea. A good journey starts long before the vehicle leaves your street. It starts with a clear conversation about who is travelling, where you want to go, when you want to leave and what would make the trip comfortable. We keep that conversation simple by giving you a website that shows every vehicle, a booking enquiry form that prepares your details, and a WhatsApp number you can message directly.

We do not publish claims about awards, ratings or years in business on this website, because we would rather you judge us by how clearly we answer your enquiry and how well the vehicle fits your plan.

Our Travel Services

As a travel service in Sankarankovil, we help customers with several kinds of journeys:

  • Family outings and holidays: weekend breaks, school-holiday trips and festival visits to relatives.
  • Group journeys: friends, colleagues, neighbourhood groups and community outings.
  • Sightseeing: day trips and multi-day routes that link viewpoints, gardens, lakes and beaches.
  • Temple visits: pilgrimages to well-known temple towns across southern Tamil Nadu.
  • Outstation travel: longer journeys across Tamil Nadu and into Kerala.

As a family travel service Sankarankovil families can reach easily on WhatsApp, we understand that a family trip often includes children, grandparents and a lot of luggage. When you enquire, tell us about the people travelling and any special needs, and we will discuss what is practical.

Our Approach to Comfortable Travel

Comfort on a long journey is not only about the seat. It is about having enough space for everyone, a vehicle that suits the road, a pickup point that saves you time, and the confidence that someone has listened to your plan.

When you contact us, we encourage you to think about the following points:

  • How many passengers are travelling, including children and elders?
  • How much luggage will you carry?
  • Will the route include hill roads, long highway stretches or crowded temple towns?
  • Do you prefer an AC vehicle for the journey?
  • Is the trip one day, or does it continue overnight?

Answering these questions in your first message helps us suggest a suitable vehicle quickly and avoids confusion later.

Journeys We Help You Plan

Most of our customers are planning one of a handful of journeys. A family wants a few relaxed days in a hill station during the school holidays. A temple group wants to visit Madurai and Rameswaram together. A group of colleagues wants a single day of sightseeing. A large family wants one vehicle for a wedding or a function in another town. Each of these journeys needs a different vehicle, a different pace and a different conversation, and we try to treat each enquiry on its own terms rather than pushing the same option to everyone.

If your journey is unusual, that is fine. Tell us what you have in mind and we will say honestly whether we can help. We would rather give you a clear answer than promise something that does not fit your plan.
